Currently if a student needs to learn anything they need to search through
the internet to find the right resource sometime videos are available
and sometime not, LLMs may generate good explanations but with plain text
and even if its a multimodal model they may generate a static image.
Books can sometimes be difficult for students to go through and some students
may find it hard to read and understand the topic.
So we have introduced TutorAI to address this specific issue faced by learners,
by leveraging the capabilities of modern LLMs.
All they have to do is type a topic they want to learn and our system will
generate an explanation about the topic with an SVG diagram to represent
the topic, and a TTS to convert generated explanation to speech. The SVGs
are tagged with specific ids that are linked to the speech. So when the
system talks about certain part of the SVG out system will also highlight
that specific part of the SVG programmatically making the learning more
interactive and easier to understand.
